01 / The problem

You build the thing.
Then comes the “what do I post?”

The work is in your commits and pull requests, but sharing it means finding the right change, putting it into words, and remembering what you actually shipped.

That extra step is easy to put off. And a generic update can say more than the code supports.

Real progress deserves an honest story↗
