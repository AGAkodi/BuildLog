BuildLog / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

BuildLog helps you turn public GitHub commits and pull requests into drafts you can review and queue through Buffer. This policy describes what BuildLog handles when you use it and how you can delete your BuildLog data.

What we collect and why

  • GitHub account information: When you sign in, BuildLog uses your GitHub account ID and username to identify your account. It creates a session so you can stay signed in. BuildLog does not store your GitHub access token.
  • Buffer connection: When you connect Buffer, BuildLog stores encrypted access and refresh tokens so it can retrieve your channels and submit posts you approve. It also uses temporary authorization information to complete sign-in and connection flows.
  • Public repository information: BuildLog requests public repository, commit, and pull request information from GitHub to list changes and prepare a draft. Do not enter a private repository; the current service supports public repositories only.
  • Drafts and submissions: Your draft is held in your browser while you work. When you queue a post, BuildLog sends the text to Buffer and stores a submission result, which may include the returned post ID, text, scheduled time, or an error. These records help prevent duplicate requests.
  • Technical information: Cloudflare provides hosting, database storage, and operational tools. Cloudflare and the service may process technical information such as request metadata and error logs to operate and troubleshoot the service.

How we use and share information

We use this information to authenticate you, show public GitHub changes, connect your Buffer account, queue posts you approve, and operate and troubleshoot BuildLog. GitHub receives requests to authenticate you and retrieve public repository information. Buffer receives the post text and channel selection when you approve a queue request. Cloudflare processes and stores data to provide the service. These providers handle information under their own policies.

How long information is kept

GitHub sessions expire after seven days; temporary authorization requests expire after ten minutes. Expired database records may remain until removed through maintenance or account deletion. Your encrypted Buffer connection is kept until you disconnect Buffer, delete your account data, or it is removed through maintenance. Submission records are kept until you delete your account data or they are removed through maintenance. A draft may remain temporarily in your browser session storage while an authorization flow completes.

Deleted information may remain in Cloudflare backups or operational logs for a period under the provider’s retention practices. Posts already sent to Buffer remain there until you remove them in Buffer.

Disconnect or delete your data

To remove BuildLog’s stored Buffer tokens, choose Disconnect in the draft studio. You can also revoke BuildLog’s access in Buffer. Disconnecting does not delete posts already queued or published through Buffer.

To delete your BuildLog account data, sign in, go to Account and privacy, and choose Delete my BuildLog data. After you confirm, BuildLog removes your stored sessions, Buffer connection, associated authorization records, and submission records. This also signs you out. It does not delete your GitHub account, your Buffer account, or posts already in Buffer. You can revoke the app’s access in GitHub and Buffer separately.

If you can’t sign in or need help with a deletion request, contact talk2monarch77@gmail.com.

Security and your choices

BuildLog encrypts stored Buffer tokens and keeps them on the server. No service can guarantee absolute security. Review drafts before approving them, avoid including sensitive information you do not want sent to Buffer, and contact us if you believe your connection has been compromised.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates when it was last updated. For privacy questions or requests, email talk2monarch77@gmail.com.
